My mail goes out through Fastmail, which sends from these three ranges:

  103.168.172.128/27     (103.168.172.128 - 103.168.172.159)
  202.12.124.128/27      (202.12.124.128  - 202.12.124.159)
  204.75.18.128/27       (204.75.18.128   - 204.75.18.159)

That's 96 addresses total. Fastmail rotates which one a given message
goes out from, so allowlisting just the single IP you banned will work
until it rotates, then break again. Better to allow all three ranges.

These come from Fastmail's published SPF record (spf.messagingengine.com),
which is the list they maintain for exactly this purpose.
